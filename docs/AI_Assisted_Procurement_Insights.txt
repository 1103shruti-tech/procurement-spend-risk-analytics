# PROCUREMENT SPEND AND RISK ANALYTICS
## Management Insights and Recommendations

### 1. Project Overview
This project analyses synthetic purchase-to-pay (P2P) procurement data, including purchase orders, vendors, receipts, invoices and payments.

The objective is to identify potential control weaknesses, improve invoice validation and support better procurement decisions.

### 2. Key Analytical Findings

**Duplicate Invoice Checks**
- 20 duplicate invoice rows were flagged.
- These represent potential duplicate submissions requiring investigation.
- Recommended action: introduce duplicate checks using invoice number, vendor ID, invoice date and invoice amount.

**Invoice Matching**
- 510 invoice records were analysed.
- 15 records were flagged for review based on invoice-to-purchase-order amount differences.
- Recommended action: automate three-way matching between purchase orders, goods receipts and invoices.

**Vendor Spend Concentration**
- Vendor V003 recorded the highest purchase-order spend of 983,439 across 34 orders.
- Its share was approximately 7.7% of total purchase-order spend.
- Recommended action: monitor vendor concentration and review high-value purchasing patterns.

### 3. Recommended Process Improvements

1. **Duplicate Invoice Prevention:** Check invoice identifiers and vendor details before accepting invoices.
2. **Automated Three-Way Matching:** Compare purchase orders, receipts and invoices before payment approval.
3. **Exception-Based Review:** Route mismatched or duplicate records to an analyst for investigation.
4. **Risk-Based Approvals:** Apply additional approval checks to high-value or unusual transactions.
5. **Vendor Monitoring:** Regularly review vendor spend, order frequency and concentration.

### 4. Suggested Management KPIs

- Total procurement spend
- Spend by vendor
- Duplicate invoice records flagged
- Invoice mismatch rate
- Purchase-order versus received quantity variance
- Invoice-to-payment processing time
- Percentage of invoices requiring manual review
